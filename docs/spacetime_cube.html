How to read this

Space, then time

The cube’s horizontal and vertical directions are measured robot coordinates. Its depth is elapsed seconds, not a distance. The front-face trace removes time to reveal the labeled digit.

The bright writing segment is an approximate X-distance crop, not contact sensing or an annotated stroke boundary.

What is measured, and what is reconstructed

Robot and trajectoryJoint angles, timestamps, and end-effector positions are from this trial. The simplified arm follows published Panda link geometry. Its joint centers are derived from the recorded angles and checked against the recorded EE positions; the extra tool-tip offset is inferred from that comparison.

Real camera imagesThe left-eye view and stills come from the selected dataset video. Video and pose share elapsed time; camera pixels are not registered to robot coordinates.

Room contextThe paper describes a 3.60 × 3.30 m lab with two arms and three sensor modules. The room view sketches that layout; object placement and the active robot’s location within it are illustrative, not surveyed or camera-calibrated.
